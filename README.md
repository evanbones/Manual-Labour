## Overview

Welcome to Manual Labour! This mod is all about bringing a bit of hands-on, hard work back to your crafting experience.
Instead of just clicking a button in a crafting table, you get to actually smash, grind, and mix your items manually.

There's a few fun new blocks to help you out. You can use the Workstone to hammer items into shape, or the Mortar
for grinding and mixing ingredients together. There's also a Millstone to grind down materials.

It's perfect for early-game, when you don't want to bother setting up your big machine assembly lines yet, or if you
just enjoy doing things the old-fashioned way. Plus, with Create installed, Manual Labour tools play nicely as manual
alternatives to their mechanical counterparts!

Check out the wiki for full details on all the items, blocks, and custom recipes.
