# Pestle

The Pestle is used together with the Mortar to grind items down by hand.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Usage

Once you have put some items into a Mortar, just hold right-click with the Pestle to start grinding. It will slowly wear down with use.

You can also shift-right-click a Mortar with a Pestle to leave it resting nicely inside.
