# Hammers

Hammers are your primary tools for processing items on the Workstone. They come in all the standard materials, from basic flint all the way up to netherite.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Usage

Grab a Hammer and right-click a Workstone to smash whatever item is resting on it. Better materials will make the Hammer last longer before it breaks.
