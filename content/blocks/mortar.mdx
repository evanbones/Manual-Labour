# Mortar

The Mortar lets you grind and mix items entirely by hand. It is perfect for early game crafting or when you just want to do things the old-fashioned way.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Usage

Right-click items onto the Mortar to add them as ingredients. Fluids can also be poured in or drawn out using buckets and bottles.

To grind the items, hold right-click on the Mortar with a Pestle. If you want to mix things instead, hold right-click with a Ladle. Each tool wears down a little bit with every use. 

When you are done, you can shift-right-click with a Pestle or Ladle to rest it decoratively on the Mortar.
