# Workstone

The Workstone is a super handy station for processing items by hand. Simply put an item on the Workstone, grab a Hammer, and give it a good whack! You will get some nice processed materials out of it.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Usage

To use the Workstone, place the block down and right-click it with the item you want to process. Then, take any Hammer and right-click the Workstone to hit the item. 

If you have Create installed, the Workstone can even act as a fallback for mechanical pressing or deploying recipes.
